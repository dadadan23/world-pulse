World Pulse // Mode Deck prototype

Interactive. Click or press 1–8 to toggle a mode. Hold a key or chip to peek (it reverts on release). Q W E R switch scenes. Space starts Showtime auto-cycle, and Showtime also starts by itself after 30 seconds without any interaction (add ?idle=5 to the URL to try it sooner); any input stops it and restores what you had on. M toggles sound (off by default). Stop touching it for six seconds and the deck collapses to a quiet LED strip. All data is illustrative.

SCENES 1–8 TOGGLE · HOLD TO PEEK
Engage feedbackCorner brackets close on the chip, LED lights, the globe takes a 60 ms glitch, a toast names the mode, and (optionally) a short rising blip plays. Off is quieter: a falling blip, no brackets.
PeekHold a key or chip to preview a mode. Release and it reverts, so trying things is never a commitment. Amber blinking LED marks a peek.
ScenesOne tap swaps a whole bundle: Ambient, Analyst, Cinema, Storm. Showtime cycles scenes on a timer for walk-by displays.
Quiet by defaultThe deck idles into a thin LED strip so the globe stays the hero. Any pointer, key or remote press wakes it.
